ZapSmith

ZapSmith plays the IPTV playlists you add yourself (Xtream Codes accounts or M3U links) on LG and Samsung TVs, Windows PCs, phones and tablets. It does not sell, host or provide any channels, films or series.

Privacy policy · Support: zakarya.atef2017@gmail.com

Demo playlist

To try ZapSmith without a provider, add this M3U link in Settings → Add a playlist:

https://zakaryaatef.github.io/zapsmith/demo.m3u

It holds public test streams and the Blender Foundation's open movies only.
